# Why Brownland is Raipur's Fastest Growing Coffee Brand

**TL;DR:** Brownland Coffee combines premium Arabica beans, local expertise, and Gen Z-friendly experiences to become Raipur's leading specialty coffee brand.

---

## The Brownland Story

Born in Raipur, Brownland Coffee started with a simple mission: bring world-class specialty coffee to Central India.

While metros enjoyed specialty coffee culture, tier-2 cities like Raipur were limited to chain cafes and instant coffee. We saw an opportunity to change that.

---

## What Makes Brownland Different

### 1. Premium Bean Selection

| Our Standard | Industry Average |
|--------------|------------------|
| 100% Arabica | Mix of Arabica/Robusta |
| Single-origin options | Blended |
| Roasted within 30 days | Often 60+ days old |
| Cupped and scored | Rarely tested |

We source from Karnataka's best estates and roast in small batches for peak freshness.
