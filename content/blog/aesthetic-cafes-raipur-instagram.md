## Photography Tips for Cafe Shots

### Equipment
- **Smartphone:** iPhone 13+ or Samsung S22+ recommended
- **Accessories:** Mini tripod, ring light for low-light
- **Apps:** Lightroom Mobile, VSCO for editing

### Composition Tips
1. **Rule of thirds** – Place main subject off-center
2. **Leading lines** – Use tables, walls to guide eye
3. **Flat lay** – Overhead shots of coffee and food
4. **Include hands** – Adds human element

### Lighting Tips
- **Golden hour** (4-6 PM) for warm outdoor shots
- **Morning light** (8-10 AM) for soft indoor shots
- **Avoid harsh noon light** – Creates unflattering shadows

## FAQ

### Which is the most Instagrammable cafe in Raipur?

Kopyko leads with its Greek-inspired aesthetics and sunset views. Cafe Selfie Land is perfect for quirky, fun content.

### Do cafes in Raipur allow photography?

Yes, all cafes welcome photography. Some even encourage it with photo spots and props.

### What's the best time for cafe photoshoots?

Golden hour (4-6 PM) for outdoor cafes. Morning (9-11 AM) for indoor spots with natural light.

### Should I ask permission before photographing?

Common courtesy is to ask if including other customers in your shots. Staff are usually happy to take photos for you.
