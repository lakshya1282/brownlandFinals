## Top 7 Date Night Cafes in Raipur

### 1. Kopyko – Sunset Romance

**Romance Rating: 10/10**

**Vibe:** Greek-inspired rooftop with stunning sunset views

**Best For:**
- First dates
- Anniversaries
- Proposal spots

**What to Order:** Sundowner drinks, Mediterranean bites

**Pro Tip:** Book the rooftop corner table an hour before sunset.

**Budget:** ₹800-1200 for two

---

### 2. Brownland Coffee – Modern Intimacy

**Romance Rating: 9/10**

**Vibe:** Minimalist, sophisticated, quiet corners

**Best For:**
- Coffee connoisseur couples
- Deep conversations
- Morning dates

**What to Order:** Pour-over for two, pastries to share

**Pro Tip:** The corner booth seating offers privacy without isolation.

**Budget:** ₹500-800 for two

---

### 3. Nukkad – Bohemian Romance

**Romance Rating: 9/10**

**Vibe:** Fairy lights, floor cushions, dreamy atmosphere

**Best For:**
- Creative couples
- Casual dates
- Late-night conversations

**What to Order:** Tea flights, sharing platters

**Pro Tip:** Visit after 8 PM when the fairy lights are fully lit.

**Budget:** ₹600-900 for two

---

### 4. The Living Room Cafe – Cozy Comfort

**Romance Rating: 8/10**

**Vibe:** Homely, lounge-style, intimate

**Best For:**
- Comfortable extended dates

- Rain day romance

**What to Order:** Full meals, coffee, desserts

**Pro Tip:** The indoor lounge area is more private than outdoor seating.

**Budget:** ₹900-1400 for two

## Date Planning Tips
