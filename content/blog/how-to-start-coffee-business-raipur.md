- **Lower Competition:** Fewer established chains than metros

## FAQ

### How much does it cost to open a coffee shop in Raipur?

Expect ₹12-33 lakhs depending on size, location, and concept. Kiosk models can start at ₹5-8 lakhs.

### How long does it take to break even?

With good location and management, 6-12 months. Premium cafes may take 12-18 months.

### Do I need barista training to open a coffee shop?

Not mandatory, but highly recommended. You should understand your product. Consider a 2-week barista course.

### What are the most common reasons cafes fail?

Poor location (35%), undercapitalization (25%), inconsistent quality (20%), bad marketing (20%).

### Can I start with a cloud kitchen model?

Yes, delivery-only coffee works for iced/cold brews. Lower investment (₹3-5L) but limited brand building.
